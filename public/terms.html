What Grocery Gitter is

Grocery Gitter helps you compare grocery prices and split a list across stores. It is not a grocery store. We do not sell the groceries, take payment for them, or deliver them. Checkout happens with the retailer you choose — Kroger, Walmart, Target, Instacart, or another store the app links to.

You can use Grocery Gitter without an account. An optional account keeps your list and alerts in sync across devices. There is no fee to use the comparison. Retailers may charge their own item, service, or delivery fees after you leave the app.

Prices can be wrong

A price may be a live shelf or catalog price, a weekly-ad price, a licensed partner feed, or a labeled demo catalog price. It can be missing, stale, or different from the register. The badge on a trip card says which kind you are looking at. Confirm the price and the product on the retailer’s site before you pay.

Walmart figures are that site’s catalog, not a promise of the price at the nearest store. Weekly-ad prices follow the circular for the ZIP you entered, not a national shelf.

Store handoffs

Buttons that open a retailer take you to that retailer’s site or app flow. Their terms and privacy policy apply there. Kroger cart login, when it is offered, is Kroger’s login. We do not receive your retailer password. A successful Kroger cart write means Kroger accepted the items; you still finish pickup or delivery on Kroger.

Instacart is offered only when that integration is turned on, and only after you tap it. Grocery Gitter does not place an Instacart order for you.

Your list, shared lists, and alerts

A list you have not shared stays on your device, as described in the privacy policy. Planning a trip sends the list and ZIP to our server for that request. That request is not stored as the list.

Share list stores the list on our server: the items, an optional display name, timestamps, and hashed share secrets. Anyone with the link can see the list, and can edit it unless you made the link view-only. Do not put a password or other secret in an item name or a display name. Revoke link turns the link off. It does not delete the stored list. Replacing the link does the same for the old address.

If you turn on price alerts, we store the watched items, the baseline price and any target price, the ZIP, and the alert records. Turning the alert checkbox off disables that watch. It does not erase the watch or past alerts. Browser notifications, when you allow them and the server is configured with VAPID keys, store a Web Push endpoint and keys so we can notify this browser.

The 5-digit ZIP may also be stored, without your name, so the catalog can refresh prices near ZIPs people use. There is no Grocery Gitter account. You can still delete the data this device stored: open Settings and choose Delete my data, then type DELETE. That removes this device’s list, alerts, notifications, shared lists you created, and the Kroger sign-in. It does not erase ZIP totals or search phrases, which are not tied to you.
